Welcome to Ada’s garden

Let’s collect nectar together! First set a team target, plant the children’s flowers, then release their butterflies.

Plant our flower garden

Choose transparent PNG or WebP cutouts of the children’s flowers. Select several files together. File names become flower names. Up to 20 flowers spread around the left, bottom and right edges, leaving the middle open for flying.

Flower heads work best; the garden adds stems for you.

Release your butterflies

Select the children’s transparent PNG or WebP cutouts together. Name each butterfly and share 10 points between its skills, then release it into the garden.

You can add more flowers and butterflies during play.
